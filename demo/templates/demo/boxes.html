A box classed hbf, Hbf, hbF or HbF stacks a header, a body and a footer. Each child names its area with mvpl-header, mvpl-body or mvpl-footer, an area left out takes no space, and an upper-case letter keeps that area in place while the box scrolls. The navigation on this page is a sidebar with view="HbF".
