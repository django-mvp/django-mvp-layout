django-mvp-layout

A header, two sidebars, the page and a footer, arranged by a nine-letter layout code. The sidebars open, close, collapse to an icon rail and become drawers on narrow screens, and a layout nests inside a card, a dialog or another layout.

The layout code

This page is laid out by lHh LpR lff. The three groups are the top, middle and bottom rows, each read left, centre and right: h header, l left sidebar, p page, r right sidebar, f footer. A corner names the area that reaches it, and an upper-case letter keeps that area in place while the page scrolls. The code reads as it does in Quasar's QLayout.

<c-layout view="lHh LpR lff">
  <c-layout.header>…</c-layout.header>
  <c-layout.sidebar side="left" collapse="icons" persist="nav">…</c-layout.sidebar>
  <c-layout.page>…</c-layout.page>
  <c-layout.sidebar side="right">…</c-layout.sidebar>
  <c-layout.footer>…</c-layout.footer>
</c-layout>

Sidebars

  • Each sidebar slides away, shrinks to an icon rail, or stays open. The navigation here shrinks to a rail.
  • Below its breakpoint it becomes a drawer over the page, measured against the layout's own width, never the window's.
  • Its open or closed state is kept in a cookie and drawn by the server, so a page never loads open and snaps shut.
  • A sidebar with nothing in it takes no space, and its toggles hide.
