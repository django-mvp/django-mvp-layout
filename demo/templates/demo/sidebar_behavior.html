Sidebar behavior

A sidebar's view stacks its content as a header, a body and a footer, written as c-layout.header, c-layout.body and c-layout.footer. Each lands in its own place whatever order it is written in, a part left out takes no space, and an upper-case letter keeps that part in place while the sidebar scrolls.
