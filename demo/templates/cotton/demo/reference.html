The letters of a layout code
LetterArea
hHeader
lLeft sidebar
pPage
rRight sidebar
fFooter

The three groups are the top, middle and bottom rows. Each group is read left, centre and right.

The letters each position takes
RowLeftCentreRight
Toph lhh r
Middlelpr
Bottomf lff r

A corner holding l or r gives that corner to the sidebar, so it runs past the header or the footer on that side.

An upper-case letter makes that area sticky: H, L, R or F.
